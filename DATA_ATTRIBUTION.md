# Data attribution

## Nemotron-Personas-Korea

The files below are fixed research samples derived from NVIDIA's
Nemotron-Personas-Korea dataset:

- `data/derived/validation_1000_locked_seed42.parquet`
- `data/derived/smoke_100_seed2026.parquet`

Source: NVIDIA Corporation, *Nemotron-Personas-Korea*, version 1.0 (2026-04-20),
<https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Personas-Korea>.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

The source dataset and these samples contain synthetic personas. The fixed
samples were selected for this study and are not human survey microdata.

## Human survey benchmark

The benchmark JSON contains a transcription of publicly reported, rounded,
weighted percentages from the Reform Institute military-service policy survey,
publication 2026-16 (survey date 2026-08-18; n=1,006).

Source URL: <https://reforminst.org/polls/poll-20260819-113339>

The original PDF is not redistributed here. The benchmark JSON records page
locations, transcription rules, and limitations. These percentages are an
empirical comparison benchmark and are not represented as error-free population
truth.
